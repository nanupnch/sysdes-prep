Use these after the guide, between study days, or in place of a mock that doesn't match your interview: a classic prompt instead of Mock B, or an LLM prompt instead of Mock A. [Mock C](#mock-c), the third full mock, is at the end of this page.

**How to run a prompt:**

1. Set a 35-minute timer. Talk out loud and draw on paper, following RESHAPE's time boxes.
2. Write your own clarifying questions and assumptions first. Then open the card and use its numbers.
3. When the timer ends, reveal the crux: the one thing a strong answer must find. If you missed it, follow the review links.

::: pitfall
Reading the crux before you try turns a test into a re-read. It feels productive and teaches far less (the testing effect). Run the prompt cold, then check.
:::

## Five questions for any design {#always}

- What is the quality bar, and how will it be measured before launch and in production?
- What happens when a part, or the model, is wrong, slow or down?
- What does a request cost at ten times the expected volume?
- Which legs of the lethal trifecta does it have, and which one does the design break?
- Who can see what the system did, and for how long?
